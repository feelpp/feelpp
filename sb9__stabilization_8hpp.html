enum class Feel::vf::detail::SB9StabilizationKind
strong

Selects one of the four SB9 mode-stabilization blocks.

These blocks correspond to the MATLAB matrices_Bs.m operators: Bs1 and Bs2 are 1x24, Bs3 is 2x24, and Bs4 is 3x24.
